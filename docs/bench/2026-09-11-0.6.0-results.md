## 6 · Retrieval

The runbook's three-way rule, unchanged, on the fixture store with every default language in it (Kotlin, SQL, HCL, Shell and Python reach `beauty-crm`), against the G15 baseline:

1. every recorded floor holds in all four arms `bench/probe/arms.sh` runs, p90 ≤ 230;
